Download for Android

Early preview for testers. The official Google Play version is coming soon.

Download

Open this page on your Android phone and tap the button. The file is about 115 MB, so use Wi-Fi if you can.

Download Legal Lexicon (.apk)

Install

1. When the download finishes, tap the file to open it.
2. If Android says it can’t install apps from this source, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source for your browser, then go back.
3. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details → Install anyway. It appears because this preview isn’t on Google Play yet.
4. Open Legal Lexicon and create your account.

Pro in the preview

Pro is free in the preview. Start the free trial on the subscription screen and no payment is taken; it shows as “Test mode”. When the app moves to Google Play, real subscriptions start there.

Updates and feedback

The preview doesn’t update by itself. Come back to this page for new versions. Found a problem or a wrong definition? Email legallexicon.support@gmail.com.
