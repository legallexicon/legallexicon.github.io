Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Who we are

Legal Lexicon is a vocabulary app for law students, operated by Legal Lexicon (Yahya Sheikh). Contact: legallexicon.support@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your email address and password (stored securely by our account provider, Supabase; we never see your password), your study progress (which entries you have studied and how well you remembered them), your settings (daily goal, reminder time, theme), sentences you choose to write, and the time you gave consent. Your progress is kept on your phone and backed up to your account so you can use it on another phone. Photos you scan are read on your phone and never uploaded. If you subscribe, Google Play processes your payment; we receive only whether your subscription is active, through our billing provider RevenueCat. We never see your card or UPI details.

AI explanations

When you ask for an AI explanation, only the word you typed (and an anonymous subscription ID, to confirm Pro) is sent to our server and on to our AI provider, Anthropic, to write the explanation. No other personal data is sent.

Why we collect it

Only to run the app: schedule your reviews, show your progress, back up your progress, send the reminder you asked for, and unlock Pro features you paid for. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run the app for us: Supabase (accounts and backup), RevenueCat and Google Play (subscriptions), and Anthropic (AI explanations, word only). Each uses the data only to provide its service to us. We disclose data to authorities only where the law requires it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Each account can read and change only its own backup.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all its data at any time: in the app, go to Settings → Delete account; or, without the app, email legallexicon.support@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. Your account, email address and backed-up progress are deleted straight away (or within 7 days of an emailed request). Copies in our hosting provider’s routine backups are overwritten within 30 days. Records of payments are kept by Google Play under its own policy.

Age

The app is for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly process children’s personal data.

Your rights

You can export your data at any time from Settings, correct it, and delete your account as described above. Deleting your account withdraws your consent. For any request or complaint, write to us at the address above; we will respond within a reasonable time. You may also approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Changes

We will tell you in the app before any material change to this policy.
