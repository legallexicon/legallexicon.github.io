Terms of Use

Using the app

Legal Lexicon is an educational tool for learning legal and academic vocabulary. You must be 18 or older to use it.

Free plan and Pro

The free plan includes 5 new entries a day and part of the word bank. Pro unlocks the full bank, the full dictionary, AI explanations, Hindi meanings, camera scan and new words added regularly, for a recurring fee shown on the subscription screen before you buy.

Free trial

New subscribers may get a 7-day free trial. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, your subscription starts automatically and you are charged the price shown. One trial per person.

Renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period until cancelled. You can cancel at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions; you keep Pro until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Refunds

Payments are processed by Google Play, and refund requests are handled under its policy. Contact us if you have a problem and we will help where we can.

Content

Study entries are written for learning and may contain errors. General-English meanings come from Open English WordNet. AI explanations are generated automatically, are labelled as AI-written, and are not reviewed before you see them. Report any error you find. Nothing in the app is legal advice (see the Content disclaimer).

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India. The courts at Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh have jurisdiction.

Content disclaimer

Educational use only. Legal Lexicon explains legal terms and maxims for learning purposes. It is not legal advice and does not create any lawyer–client relationship. For advice on a specific situation, consult a qualified advocate.


Accuracy. We check study entries carefully, but language and law change. AI explanations are generated automatically and may be wrong. Always confirm important points against primary sources such as statutes and judgments.
