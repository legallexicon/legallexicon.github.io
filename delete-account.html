Delete your Legal Lexicon account

Last updated 31 October 2026

In the app

Open Legal Lexicon → Settings → Delete account, and confirm. Your account and all its data are deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email legallexicon.support@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete your account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Your account, email address, password and backed-up study progress. Copies in our hosting provider’s routine backups are overwritten within 30 days.

What is not deleted

If you have a subscription, cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions; deleting your account does not cancel it. Google keeps its own payment records under its policy.
